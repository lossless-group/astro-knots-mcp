## Why Care?

Step 0 used to ask three questions in one message and start building. Now it
grills the person until there's a signed-off narrative, then builds a mood
board from it, so the site's copy and look come from decisions they made.

## What's New?

- **Step 0 of `start-an-astro-knot`** runs `write-site-narrative`, then
  `build-a-moodboard`. Machine and plugin setup can happen around them.
- **The build table** now takes `DESIGN.md` and `/brand-kit` from the mood
  board's decisions, and writes page copy as Markdown from the narrative.
- **Two skills synced in** from lossless-agent-skills, so the plugins and the
  Worker carry them: 12 skills in all.
