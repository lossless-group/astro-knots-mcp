## Why Care?

The astro-knots splash, the page selling these patterns, rendered its notes
and changelog as titles over empty bodies. knot-check would only have caught
it because LFM wasn't installed at all. A site with LFM installed but
rendering through Astro's `render()` would have passed.

## What's New?

- **`empty-article-body` (error):** a built page whose main article (the one
  holding its h1) has nothing after its header. Calibrated on eight of our
  sites: it flags the old splash's 225 empty pages and eight genuinely empty
  pages on fullstack-vc, and nothing else.
- **`code-unhighlighted` (warning):** a `CodeBlock` that doesn't call
  `@lossless-group/lfm/highlight`. Six of our sites haven't adopted it yet.
- **The setup playbook states it as a rule:** content renders through LFM,
  code is colored by LFM's highlighter, and a built page is opened to confirm
  its body has text.
- **The synced `lossless-flavored-markdown` skill** now carries the same hard
  rules and install steps that work.
- **41 tests.**
